I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, advised by Professor Junxian He, and a Research Intern at MINIMAX. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My current research interests include LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models, and truthfulness and interpretability of large language models.
